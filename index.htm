How an enquiry is handled

From your message to a fix, in the order it happens.

  1. You send the form

    Name, email and a description of the problem. That is all we ask for.

  2. We read it

    A technician reviews your enquiry and works out what is needed.

  3. We reply by email

    You get an answer or a next step at the address you provided.
